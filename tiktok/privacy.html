Privacy Policy

tkws-studio · last updated 26 October 2026

tkws-studio is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the "90's Legend" channel to upload the channel's own finished short videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

What we collect

When the channel owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool:

  • basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar), to show which account is connected;
  • an access token and a refresh token, to upload videos to that account.

We do not collect information about viewers, followers or any other TikTok user.

How we use it

The information is used only to upload the channel's own videos to the connected account (as drafts to review and post in the TikTok app). It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or given to anyone else.

Where it is stored

Tokens and account information are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a private file that is not uploaded to any server or shared service.

Removing your data

You can disconnect at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or in the tool itself. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message the 90's Legend channel on TikTok (@tkws2).
