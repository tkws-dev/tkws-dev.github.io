Privacy Policy

tkws-studio · YouTube · last updated 27 October 2026

tkws-studio is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the "90's Legend" YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own finished Shorts to that channel. It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

What we collect

When the channel owner connects their Google / YouTube account, Google gives the tool:

  • basic channel information (channel ID, title, avatar), to show which channel is connected;
  • an access token and a refresh token, to upload and schedule videos on that channel.

We do not collect information about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

How we use it

The information is used only to upload the channel's own Shorts to the connected channel (as private videos with a scheduled publish time). It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or given to anyone else.

Where it is stored

Tokens and account information are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a private file that is not uploaded to any server or shared service.

Removing your data

You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account (Security → Third-party access) or disconnect inside the tool itself. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message the 90's Legend channel on YouTube (@90sLegend-tkws).
